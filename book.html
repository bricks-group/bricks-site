Before you book.

Add-ons, scheduling, and what happens if plans change.

What's included with twilight?

Exterior photography shot during blue hour — windows lit, sky still holding color. It's an optional add-on (+$150–200 by footprint) on any shoot; pick it in Step 2.

What is virtual staging?

Furniture and décor added to your empty or dated interior photos in post — no physical staging, no movers. It's priced per image staged at $35 / image; tell us how many rooms to stage in the access notes and we'll confirm the count before delivery.

Can I reschedule after booking?

More than 48 hours before the shoot window is free. Inside 48 hours we will try; if we cannot, the cancellation schedule applies. Email booking@brxs.ca as soon as you know — see the cancellation policy.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 48 hours before the shoot window: full refund. 48 hours down to 4 hours: 50% refund. Inside 4 hours, or no-show / no access: no refund (100% retained). If we cancel on our side: full refund or a free reshoot, your choice. Full schedule in the Terms.

How do refunds work?

Back to your original payment method via Stripe, typically within 5–10 business days.
