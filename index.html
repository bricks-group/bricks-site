Questions

Before you book.

Is the platform live?

No — the media service is live in the GTA today; the Booking app, Dispatch network, and Brokerage OS are in development. You can book media now via the form above or email.

How do I book a shoot?

Use the booking form (it emails us pre-filled) or write booking@brxs.ca. We confirm the slot and send the one-pager with exact pricing by footprint.

What's included?

HDR photo, licensed drone, and a floor plan every shoot. Cinematic reel and 3D virtual tour are optional add-ons (+$99–179 and +$149). Build your shoot à la carte on the pricing page.

Where do you shoot?

The Greater Toronto Area. Outside GTA, ask — we'll tell you if it's covered.

When do I get my media?

Edited and MLS-ready in 24–48 hours.
