  "book direct," not "subscribe / member discount / credits." Those claims belong to bricks-site,
  not this prelaunch site.
- Tagline: "the operating system for real estate — From Capture to Close."
- Differentiator vs any photographer: the operating system layer (one standard, booked in one place,
  consistent across a whole book), NOT "we bundle photo+drone+floorplan" (every RE shooter does).
