# GTA Real-Estate Media — Competitor Rate Research (2026)

Sources pulled 2026-07-22 (web). All CAD, exclude HST unless noted. Used to
ground Bricks' à la carte pricing so it sits competitively in the GTA market.

## Competitors & headline pricing
- **REPhotos** — à la carte leader. Photo Essentials $149 (S) → $229 (XL). Floor plan $99 flat.
  3 drone photos $39, 10 drone $99, drone video $99, drone+video $179. Matterport $149.
  Reel $149. Travel: free within Halton/Peel; $0.47/km after 30 km.
- **Dee Visuals** — packages $375–$1055 by size. HDR-only from $200. iGUIDE tour from $225.
  Aerial photos $90 / video $90 / combo $150. Dusk photo $225. Travel free ≤50km, $25–50 beyond.
- **Dreamhouse Photo** — Photo $189, Floor plan $149, Drone photo $149, Drone video $199,
  Drone photo+video $299. Packages $289–$979.
- **Green Day Photography** — Package A $180, B $260, C $420. Drone add-on $100.
- **Rise Real Estate Media** — Photo $200–$235. Video tour $300–$400. Aerial video $325.
  iGUIDE $300–$375. Combos $495–$525.
- **Estate Photography** — Photo $229–$329 by sqft. Video $249–$449. iGuide $219–$319.
  Twilight from $175.
- **SnapScene** — Aerial photos $150, aerial+video $250. Twilight $200. Floor plan $125.
- **HDR E Photo** — Essential pkg $425, Premium $725, Luxury $1,150. Reel add-on $150.
  Aerial add-on $125. Twilight $200 (real) / $25 img (digital).

## Notes
- Most GTA agents buy a BUNDLE ($349–$599 typical), not pure à la carte. Bricks'
  à la carte menu is valid but the "How your quote is built" framing should also
  hint that combining services is normal (optional bundle later).
- Bricks differentiators already match market: floor plan included, 24–48h,
  cinematic reel as a tier, licensed drone.
- Bricks travel (25km free, $0.75/km) is slightly above REPhotos' $0.47/km but
  within market norms; defensible.
- Mid-market positioning: price at/ just below the "typical" column to win on
  value without racing to the $149 floor.
