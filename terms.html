2. Booking and payment

You pay the quoted amount in full, including HST, at checkout via Stripe to lock the slot. The quote shown at pay is the contract price for the selected package and add-ons. Prices are CAD. We do not store full card numbers.

3. Scheduling

After payment, the selected time is held. We confirm within one business day. If a slot cannot be honoured on our side, we will reschedule or refund per section 5.

Edited media is typically delivered within 24–48 hours of the shoot. Times are targets, not guarantees. Weather, airspace, site access, equipment failure, or third-party processing can delay. Deliverables are licensed as in section 6. Download and keep copies. We are not a long-term archive once your files have been delivered.

5. Reschedule, cancellation, refunds

Contact booking@brxs.ca as soon as you know.

Reschedule more than 48 hours before the shoot window: free.

Reschedule inside 48 hours: we will try; if we cannot, section 5 cancellation rules apply.

Your cancellation (or no-access / no-show on your side):

  • 48 hours down to 4 hours before: 50% refund.
  • Inside 4 hours, or no-show / no access: no refund.

If we cancel, fail to attend, or cannot complete the shoot for reasons on our side (including weather that makes a safe shoot impossible and no reschedule you accept): full refund or a free reshoot, your choice.
