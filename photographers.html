Before you apply

What to expect.

How long does review take?

We review every application manually. Portfolio quality is the primary gate — sharp composition, accurate colour, consistent exposure across a full property set. Expect a response within 1–3 business days.

What equipment do I need?

Full-frame or equivalent, fast primes, and ideally a DJI/Autel drone if you're applying for aerial work. List your kit in the application.

Do I need drone certification?

For aerial jobs, yes — GTA controlled airspace (Pearson / Billy Bishop) requires Transport Canada Advanced RPAS certification. We verify before routing any drone job.

Am I an employee?

No — you're an independent contractor under Ontario law, contracting with Bricks Group. You control equipment, methods, and availability. Insurance and WSIB proof are required before a first dispatched job — see the IC agreement.
