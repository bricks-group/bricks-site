Raphael Merchant

Founder · Bricks

Raphael Merchant started Bricks to give GTA listing agents one consistent standard of media — HDR photography, licensed drone, floor plans, and 3D virtual tours — booked direct, delivered in 24–48 hours.

Before Bricks, Raphael shot and profiled 14,000+ properties as a regional creative lead at CoStar — architectural photography and portfolio work at a volume most photographers never reach. That work helped power Apartments.com's launch across the Greater Golden Horseshoe, which aired during Super Bowl LVIII. That volume is where the standard comes from: accurate, measured, and built to move listings — not just look pretty.

Raphael also holds a Transport Canada Advanced RPAS (Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems) certificate, which is what lets Bricks fly legally for commercial aerial work in the complex airspace around Toronto and Mississauga — including near Pearson.
