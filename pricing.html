Pricing questions

Before you book.

How is footprint defined?

Small / Mid / Large / XL map to the property's finished area and complexity. Not sure which tier? Pick "advise me" in booking and we'll size it from the address.

Is the 3D tour included?

No — it's a +$149 add-on on any tier. Floor plan is included from Standard; twilight is included from Luxury.

Are these the final platform rates?

These are the pre-launch direct-booking rates for the GTA. The platform's own rate card may differ when Booking goes live.

Do you shoot outside the GTA?

Ask — we'll tell you if it's covered and quote travel if needed.
