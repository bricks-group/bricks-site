How your quote is built

Your price is assembled from these factors — we only charge for what your listing actually needs:

  • Footprint — finished area & complexity (Small / Mid / Large / XL) sets the photography & drone base.
  • What you include — add only the services you want (reel, 3D tour, twilight, rush). Nothing you don't.
  • Travel — see below. Shooting inside 25 km of Mississauga is free.
  • Volume — agents and teams booking regularly get per-shoot rates tuned to their pipeline. Ask.

Footprint tiers: Small ≈ ≤1,200 sq ft · Mid ≈ 1,200–2,500 · Large ≈ 2,500–4,000 · XL ≈ 4,000+. Not sure which you are? Pick "advise me" in booking and we size it from the address — no guesswork, no overcharge.

Travel

Base is 6330 Montevideo Rd, Mississauga. The first 25 km is free. Beyond that, travel is $0.75 / km, rounded to the nearest km and quoted up front before you confirm. Example: a shoot 40 km out = 15 km billable × $0.75 = $11.25. Outside the GTA, ask and we'll tell you if it's covered and quote travel before you book.

Taxes: all prices are in CAD, exclude HST (13%), which is added at checkout. Estimates, not ceilings: the grid above is the à la carte menu — your final quote is the sum of what you actually book, so you never pay for a tier you didn't need. The self-serve Booking app will show your live total as you build the shoot.
